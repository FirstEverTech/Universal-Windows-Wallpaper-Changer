[Cyan] SUPPORT DEVELOPMENT
[Cyan] ===================

 Universal Windows Wallpaper Changer sets a fresh wallpaper on Windows
 from free image services (FirstEverTech, Bing, Picsum, Wallhaven),
 your own folders, or your own links - once, or on a schedule in the
 background with a tray icon.

 No administrator rights are needed and the tool is free and
 open-source. The provider integrations, background mode and the
 image filtering pipeline are maintained in my free time.

 If this tool saved you time or made your desktop a little nicer,
 please consider supporting development.
